Project Overview
This case study analyses the website structure, SEO strategy, and conversion architecture of an IT recycling and data destruction company operating in the UK environmental services sector.
The goal of the audit was to evaluate the site's ability to generate organic traffic and propose a search-driven content architecture capable of scaling to 60k–100k monthly organic traffic.
The analysis focuses on three major areas:
Search Engine Optimisation (SEO)
User Experience (UX)
Conversion & Lead Generation
The website currently operates primarily as a brochure-style informational site rather than a search-led lead generation platform.
This audit proposes a structured strategy to transform it into a high-performance SEO growth engine.
Objectives
The key objectives of this audit were:
Analyse the existing website architecture
Identify SEO and UX issues limiting growth
Propose a scalable SEO content architecture
Improve lead generation and conversion pathways
Build topical authority in the IT recycling industry
Expected outcomes include:
Increased organic search visibility
Stronger topical authority
Improved trust signals and compliance communication
Higher enquiry and lead generation rates
